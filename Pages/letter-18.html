When You Want To Read My Heart

Meri jaan... 🫂❤️

Agar aapne ye letter khola hai, toh shayad aaj aap mera dil padhna chahte hain.

Toh aaj main kuch hide nahi karungi. Jo dil me hai, woh sab keh deti hu. 🥹

Aap mere liye bohot special hain. Itne special ki kabhi kabhi mujhe khud samajh nahi aata ki main itna kuch feel kaise kar leti hu.

Aapki ek chhoti si baat mera pura mood change kar sakti hai. Aapki ek smile mujhe bina kisi reason ke khush kar sakti hai.

Aur jab aap pareshan hote hain, mera mann karta hai bas aapke paas baithu, aapko hug karu aur bolu— “Bas... main hu na.” 🫂

Mujhe nahi pata future me hum kitni memories aur banayenge, kitni fights hongi, kitni baar ek dusre ko tang karenge, aur kitni baar hasenge...

Lekin ek cheez mujhe pata hai— aapki presence mere liye matter karti hai.

Bohot zyada.

Main chahti hu ki aap jab bhi mere baare me sochein, aapko ek aisi feeling aaye jisme thoda sa sukoon ho, thodi si smile ho, aur bohot saara pyaar ho. 🤍

Aur agar kabhi meri kisi baat se aapko hurt hua ho, toh I am sorry. Main perfect nahi hu, but meri feelings aapke liye bilkul real hain.

Aap mere liye sirf words, chats ya memories nahi hain... aap mere dil ka ek bohot pyaara sa hissa hain. ❤️

Isliye jab bhi doubt ho, jab bhi meri feelings ko lekar confusion ho, bas ye letter dobara padh lena.

Aur yaad rakhna— meri jaan ko uski mumma bohot, bohot, bohot pyaar karti hai. 🫂💋

Muaaaaaaw meri jaan. ❤️

Forever with you,

🤍 Mummaa
