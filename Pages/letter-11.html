When You Need To Feel Loved

Mera shona 🫂🤍

Agar kabhi aapko lage ki aapko thoda sa extra pyaar chahiye, toh ye letter khol lena.

Kyunki main chahti hu ki aapko kabhi bhi ye doubt na ho ki aap kitne special hain. 🥺💕

Aap mere liye sirf ek person nahi hain jisse main baat karti hu. Aap meri life ka woh beautiful part hain jiske hone se bohot kuch aur achha lagta hai.

Aapki choti choti baatein, aapki random si bakwaas, aapka mujhe tang karna, aur woh saare moments jo shayad aapke liye normal hain... mere liye bohot precious hain. 🫂💕

Main shayad har baar perfect words me nahi bata paati, lekin believe me, I love you more than I know how to explain.

Isliye jab kabhi aapko lage ki duniya bohot noisy ho gayi hai, ya aapko bas kisi ki zarurat hai... toh meri taraf aa jaiyega. 🤍

Yahan aapko kuch prove nahi karna. Kuch pretend nahi karna. Bas aapko aap rehna hai.

Aur meri taraf se aapko ek unlimited supply of hugs, kisses aur pyaar milta rahega. 🤭🫂💋

Toh please kabhi ye mat sochna ki aap alone hain.

Aap bohot loved hain. Bohot zyada. 🤍

Aur haan... ye letter jab bhi kholenge, ek extra hug automatically add ho jayegi. 🫂💕

Muaaaaaaw 💋

I love you, meri jaan. 🤍

With all my love,

🤍 Mummaa
