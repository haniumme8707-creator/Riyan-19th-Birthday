When You Can't Sleep

Mera baccha 🌙🫂

Agar aap raat ko ye letter padh rahe hain aur neend aapke paas aane ka naam hi nahi le rahi, toh idhar aaiye. 🤭

Phone thoda side me rakhiye, comfortable ho jaiye, aur ek baar deep breath lijiye. 🤍

Ab imagine kijiye ki main aapke paas baithi hu aur hum dono bina kisi hurry ke bas raat ki shaanti enjoy kar rahe hain. 🫂

Shayad main aapko random random baatein suna rahi hoti, aur aap mujhe bol rahe hote— “Mumma, so jaiye ab.” 😭😂

Aur main obviously kehti— “Pehle aap soiye.” 🤭

🎙️ Aapko yaad hai na... meri lori sunte hi aap so jaate hain. 🥹🫂

Toh aaj bhi meri taraf se ek chhoti si lori. 🌙🤍

Ab aankhein band kijiye... aur bas meri awaaz sunte hue so jaiye. 🫂💕

Toh ab meri baat maaniye... kal ki tension kal dekhenge. Jo cheezein abhi solve nahi ho sakti, unhe abhi solve karne ki zarurat bhi nahi hai.

Bas aankhein band kijiye aur sochiye ki aap safe hain, loved hain, aur koi hai jo aapki fikar karta hai. 🥹🤍

Ab meri taraf se ek goodnight kiss 💋 aur ek bohot tight hug. 🫂🫂💕

Goodnight meri jaan. Ab chup-chaap so jaiye. 😗🌙

Sweet dreams, my love. 🤍

With all my love,

🤍 Mummaa
