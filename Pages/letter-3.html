Pata hai, kabhi kabhi mujhe lagta hai ki happiness actually bohot choti-choti cheezon me chhupi hoti hai. 😗👉🏻👈🏻 Pr pta h sabse Jyada khushi kab hoti h jab me apke sath hoti hu 🥹

Kabhi aapki koi random si baat, kabhi aapka bina reason mujhe tease karna, kabhi humari woh silly si conversations… aur kabhi bas aapka mere saath hona. 🫂🤍

Mujhe nahi pata aapko realize hai ya nahi, lekin aap meri life ke un logon me se hain jinke saath normal sa din bhi special lagne lagta hai. 🥺💕

Aapke saath mujhe kisi fancy plan ki zarurat nahi hoti. Bas aap ho, thodi si bak-bak ho, thodi si masti ho, aur hum dono apni hi duniya me. 🤭🫂

Aur mujhe humari ye choti-choti moments bohot precious lagti hain. Shayad future me hum inhi random moments ko yaad karke bolenge—

“Yaad hai hum kitne pagal the?” 😭😂💕

I hope life aapko bohot saari khushiyan de. Aise moments de jahan aapko kisi cheez ki tension na ho, bas dil halka ho aur aap genuinely khush ho. 🤍

Aur jab bhi aapke paas koi bohot achha moment ho na, please mujhe usme thoda sa include kar lena. 🤭🫂

Chahe ek photo bhej kar, ek random message karke, ya bas itna kehkar—

“Mumma, aaj bohot achha din tha.” 🥹💗

Mujhe aapki life ke ye little moments bhi sunne hain. Aapki boring stories bhi. 😂 Aapki random bakwaas bhi. Aapke plans bhi. Aapke dreams bhi. Sab kuch. 🫂🤍

Aur haan… kabhi kabhi agar life thodi difficult ho jaye, toh ye mat bhoolna ki aapko har cheez akela handle nahi karna. 🥺

Main hoon na. 🫂💕
