When You Need A Smile

Mera shona 🤭🫂💕

Oiii... kya hua? 👀 Kisne meri jaan ka mood kharab kar diya?

Jaldi bataiye mujhe. 😤 Main abhi usse complaint karne ja rahi hu. 😂

Lekin agar reason ye hai ki aapka mood bas randomly off hai, toh bhi tension lene ki zarurat nahi hai. 🫂🤍

Kyunki aapke paas ek certified pagal ladki hai jo aapko smile karwane ke liye kuch bhi kar sakti hai. 🤭😂

Toh chaliye... pehla treatment: ek bohot tight hug. 🫂🫂🫂

Doosra treatment: ek muaaaaaawwwah. 💋

Aur teesra treatment... meri bakwaas. 😭😂

Waise aapko pata hai? Aap mujhe jitna serious dikhne ki koshish karte hain na, mujhe utna hi mann karta hai aapko aur tang karu. 🤭

Main imagine kar rahi hu aap ye letter padh rahe hain aur soch rahe hain—

“Ye ladki kabhi serious nahi ho sakti.” 😭

Bilkul sahi soch rahe hain. 😂

Lekin ek serious baat bolu? Aapko sad dekhna mujhe bilkul pasand nahi hai. 🥺

Isliye jab bhi life thodi boring, irritating ya annoying lage, bas yaad rakhna... kahin na kahin ek mumma hai jo aapko bina reason bhi smile karwana chahti hai. 🫂💕

Aur agar kuch bhi kaam na kare... toh mujhe call kar lena. 🤭 Main itni bakwaas karungi ki aapko eventually hasna hi padega. 😂😂

Ab ek kaam kijiye... phone ko thoda door rakhiye, ek deep breath lijiye, aur...

SMILE PLEASE. 😗👉🏻😁

Haan bas! Aise hi. 🤭💕

Aur agar abhi smile aa gayi hai, toh mission accomplished. 😌🫂

Muaaaaaaw 💋

Ab meri jaan ka mood kharab nahi hona chahiye. Samjhe? 😤💕

With all my love,

🤍 Mummaa
