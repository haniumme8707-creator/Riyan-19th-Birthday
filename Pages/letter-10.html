Mera gudda 🫂🤍

Agar aapko abhi meri hug yaad aa rahi hai, toh idhar aaiye... aaj ki hug thodi extra tight hai. 🫂🫂💕

Bas aankhein band kijiye aur imagine kijiye ki main aapke bilkul paas hoon.

Main aapko hug karke kuch der ke liye bilkul nahi chhod rahi. 🥺🫂

Aapko pata hai na, hugs ki bhi alag-alag types hoti hain? 🤭

Ek normal wali, ek “I missed you” wali, ek “sab theek ho jayega” wali... aur ek woh wali jisme bas mann karta hai saamne wale ko pakad kar rakho aur kuch bhi mat bolo. 🤍

Aapko meri taraf se woh last wali hug mil rahi hai. 🫂💕

No questions. No explanations. No worrying. Bas aap aur meri jhappi. 🥹🤍

Kaash main sach me abhi aapke paas hoti... toh shayad main aapko itni der tak hug karti ki aap khud bol dete— “Mumma bas bhi kijiye.” 😭😂

Aur main obviously bolti— “Nahi. Aaj nahi chhodungi.” 🤭🫂

Jab bhi aapko meri yaad aaye, please ye mat sochna ki aap mujhse door hain.

Bas ye sochna ki kahin na kahin meri ek bohot tight jhappi aapka wait kar rahi hai. 💗

Aur jab hum next time milenge na... toh ye saari missed hugs ek saath collect kar lungi. 🤭🫂🫂🫂

Tab koi excuse nahi chalega. Bohot saari hugs. Bohot saari baatein. Aur shayad thode se aansu bhi. 🥹💕

Tab tak... whenever you miss my hug, just come back to this letter.

Ye letter meri taraf se ek virtual jhappi hai. 🫂🤍

Tight wali. Bohot tight wali. 🫂🫂💕
