While Listening To Our Song

Meri jaan 🎧🫂💕

Agar aap ye letter humara song sunte hue padh rahe hain, toh ek second ke liye sab kuch side me rakhiye aur bas song feel kijiye. 🥹🤍

Pata hai, mujhe nahi lagta tha ki koi random sa song kabhi itna special ho sakta hai.

Lekin ab jab bhi Until I Found You bajta hai na, pata nahi kyun sabse pehle aap yaad aate hain. 🥺💕

Kuch songs bas songs nahi rehte... woh kisi person se, kisi feeling se, kisi memory se jud jaate hain.

Aur mere liye ye song ab thoda sa aap hai. 🫂🤍

Kabhi kabhi main sochti hu, agar hum dono ek saath kahin baithe hote aur ye song play ho raha hota, toh shayad main kuch bolti hi nahi. 🤭

Bas aapke paas baithi rehti, aapko dekhti, aur secretly ye moment apne mind me save kar leti. 🥹💗

Aur shayad thodi der baad aap mujhe dekh kar bolte—

“Kya dekh rahi hain?” 🤭

Aur main obviously bolti— “Kuch bhi toh nahi.” 😗😂

Lekin sach ye hai... mujhe aapko dekhna kabhi boring nahi lagega. 🫂💕

So whenever this song plays, thodi der ke liye mujhe yaad kar lena.

Aur imagine karna ki main aapke paas hoon, aapka haath pakde hue, aur hum dono bas quietly song sun rahe hain. 🤍🫂

Maybe someday hum actually aise baith kar ye song sunenge... bina kisi screen ke, bina kisi distance ke. 🥹💕

Aur us din main aapko zaroor bolungi—

“Dekha? Maine kaha tha na, ye humara song hai.” 🤭💗

Tab tak... whenever you hear it, consider it a little reminder of me. 🫂💕

Muaaaaaaw 💋

I love you meri jaan. 🤍

With all my love,

🤍 Mummaa
