Meri jaan 🤍

Agar aap ye letter apni dua ke baad padh rahe hain, toh sabse pehle ek kaam kijiye... thoda sa smile kijiye. 🫂💕

Aaj jo bhi aapne Allah se maanga hai, jo bhi dil ki baat kahi hai, I hope usme se har woh cheez aapko mile jo aapke liye behtar ho. 🤲🏻🤍

Aur agar kabhi aap kisi cheez ko lekar worried ho, toh please yaad rakhiye... har cheez ka apna ek waqt hota hai.

Kabhi answer turant milta hai, kabhi thoda wait karna padta hai, aur kabhi baad me samajh aata hai ki jo hua wahi sabse achha tha.

Bas apna dil halka rakhiye aur umeed mat chhodiye. 🤍

Aur haan... meri ek chhoti si dua bhi hai. 🥹

Allah aapko hamesha khush rakhe, aapko har mushkil se bachaye, aapke dil ko sukoon de, aur aapke chehre ki ye pyaari si smile hamesha bani rahe. 🫂💕

Aap jitna deserve karte hain, usse bhi zyada khushiyan milein. Aur jab bhi life thodi difficult lage, aapko strength mile usse face karne ki.

Aur meri taraf se... ek chhoti si extra dua: humari ye bonding hamesha aise hi pyaari rahe. 🤍

Ameen. 🤲🏻

Ab jaiye meri jaan, dil halka kijiye, aur ek bohot pyaari si smile kijiye. 🫂💕
