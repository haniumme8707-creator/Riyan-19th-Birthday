After Seeing Our Memories

Mera gudda 🫂🤍

Agar aap ye letter humari memories dekhne ke baad padh rahe hain, toh mujhe pata hai aapke mind me bhi abhi bohot saari cheezein chal rahi hongi. 🥹💕

Photos aur videos dekhte hue na, kabhi kabhi mujhe khud believe nahi hota ki humne itne saare moments saath me jeeye hain.

Kuch memories itni choti hain ki shayad us waqt humne unhe seriously notice bhi nahi kiya... lekin aaj jab unhe dobara dekhti hu, toh woh mere liye bohot precious lagti hain. 🤍

Ek photo, ek random conversation, ek silly moment, ek stupid si smile... pata nahi kitni saari choti-choti cheezein humari story ka part ban gayi hain. 🫂

Aur sabse funny baat pata hai kya? 🤭 Humne kabhi baith kar nahi socha tha ki hum itni saari memories bana lenge.

Bas ek din ke baad doosra din aata gaya, hum baatein karte gaye, haste rahe, ladte rahe, manate rahe... aur pata hi nahi chala kab ye sab itna special ho gaya. 🥹💕

Kabhi kabhi main future ke baare me sochti hu aur imagine karti hu ki ek din hum aaj ki photos dekh kar kitna hasenge. 😂

Shayad hum bolenge— “Hum itne bacche kyun the?” 😭😂

Aur phir shayad humari aankhon me thodi si happiness bhi hogi, kyunki hum jaanenge ki humne kitna beautiful time saath me spend kiya tha. 🤍

Isliye agar kabhi aapko lage ki koi moment ordinary hai, toh usse thoda sa enjoy kar lena. Ho sakta hai wahi moment kuch saal baad humari favourite memory ban jaye. 🫂💕

Thank you meri jaan, meri life me itni saari beautiful memories add karne ke liye. 🥹🤍

Aur abhi toh bohot kuch baaki hai... bohot saari photos, bohot saari stories, bohot saari jagah, aur bohot saare moments jo hume abhi jeene hain. 💗

So please... camera ready rakhiye. 🤭📸 Humari memory gallery abhi bohot lambi hone wali hai.

Muaaaaaaw 💋🫂

Here's to all the memories we've made and all the ones waiting for us. 🤍💕

With all my love,

🤍 Mummaa
