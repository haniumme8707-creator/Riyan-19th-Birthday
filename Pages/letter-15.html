Before You Go To Sleep

Meri jaan, mera gudda 🌙🫂

Sone se pehle bas do minute meri baat sun lijiye. 🤭

Aaj ka din jaisa bhi raha ho— acha, boring, tiring, ya thoda sa difficult... ab usse chhod dijiye.

Jo ho gaya, woh ho gaya. Kal ek naya din hoga, aur aapko usse fresh start karna hai. 🤍

Abhi bas apne aap ko thoda rest dijiye. Aapko har waqt strong rehne ki zarurat nahi hai.

Aur agar aaj kisi baat ne aapko hurt kiya, toh please usse lekar raat bhar mat sochiye. Aapki peace usse zyada important hai. 🫂

Main chahti hu ki jab aap aankhein band karein, toh aapke mind me tension nahi, bas sukoon ho.

Aur agar meri yaad aaye... toh smile kar dena. 🤭💕 Kyunki main bhi kahin na kahin aapko yaad karke smile kar rahi houngi.

Ab phone side me rakhiye, blanket me comfortably ghusiye, aur aankhein band kijiye. 😭🫂

Meri taraf se ek bohot tight hug 🫂 aur forehead kiss 💋

Goodnight meri jaan. Kal phir milenge. 🌙🤍

Sweet dreams, mera baccha. 💕

With all my love,

🤍 Mummaa
