When You Think About Us

Meri jaan 🫂🤍

Jab bhi aap humare baare me sochte hain na, I hope aapke face par ek choti si smile zaroor aati ho. 🥹💕

Kyunki jab main humare baare me sochti hu, mere mind me ek saath bohot saari cheezein aa jaati hain.

Humari random si baatein, humari silly fights, ek dusre ko chidhana, phir mana lena... aur woh saare little moments jo sirf hum dono samajhte hain. 🤭🫂

Kabhi kabhi main sochti hu ki hum dono kitne alag hain, phir bhi pata nahi kaise ek dusre ke saath itne comfortable ho gaye. 🤍

Aap meri life ka woh part ban gaye hain jiske bina ab normal days bhi thode incomplete se lagte hain. 🥺💕

Aur jab main future ke baare me sochti hu, toh mujhe bohot saare simple moments imagine hote hain.

Saath me chai peena, bina kisi reason ke bahar nikal jaana, ek dusre ko random si stories sunana, aur phir raat ko baith kar “aaj ka din kaisa tha” discuss karna. 🫂☕

Mujhe grand things se zyada ye little things chahiye. Bas aap, main, aur humari apni choti si duniya. 🥹🤍

Aur haan... hum perfect nahi hain, na hi humesha sab kuch perfect hoga.

Kabhi arguments honge, kabhi mood off hoga, kabhi ek dusre ko samajhne me time lagega. Lekin I hope hum har baar ek dusre ko choose karenge. 🫂💕

Kyunki mere liye “us” sirf achhe moments ka naam nahi hai.

“Us” ka matlab hai — har situation me ek dusre ka saath. 🤍

So whenever you think about us, sirf past yaad mat karna... thoda sa future bhi imagine karna. 🤭💗

Kyunki mujhe lagta hai humari story ke best parts abhi likhe hi nahi gaye. 🥹🫂💕

Muaaaaaaw 💋

I love our little “us”. 🤍

With all my love,

🤍 Mummaa
