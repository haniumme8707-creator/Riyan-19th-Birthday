When You're Tired

Hii meri jaan 🫂🤍

Agar aap ye letter tab padh rahe hain jab aap bohot tired hain, toh sabse pehle... thoda sa rest kijiye. 🥺🫂

Har waqt strong rehna zaroori nahi hota. Kabhi kabhi bas ruk jaana, aankhein band karna, aur thodi der kuch na karna bhi zaroori hai. 🤍

Isliye abhi apne aap ko bilkul bhi pressure mat dijiye. Jo kaam baad me ho sakta hai, woh baad me kar lenge.

Abhi bas comfortable ho jaiye, thoda paani pee lijiye, aur agar possible ho toh meri taraf se ek bohot tight hug le lijiye. 🫂🫂💕

Kaash main abhi aapke paas hoti... toh shayad aapko kuch bolne bhi nahi deti. 🤭 Bas aapko apne paas bithati aur kehti—

“Bas baba, ab thoda rest kar lijiye. Baaki sab baad me.” 🥺🫂

Aur agar aaj ka din difficult raha hai, toh please yaad rakhiye... ek difficult day ka matlab ye bilkul nahi hota ki aapne kuch galat kiya hai.

Aap jitna kar sakte hain, utna karna bhi enough hai. 🤍

Mujhe aap par bohot proud hai. Aapko shayad hamesha khud nahi dikhta, lekin mujhe dikhta hai. 🥹💕

Toh ab meri baat maaniye... phone thoda side me rakhiye, aankhein band kijiye, aur ek deep breath lijiye. 🫂

Imagine kijiye main aapke paas hoon aur aapko ek warm sa hug de rahi hoon. 💗

Aaj ke liye itna hi. Baaki duniya ko thodi der wait karne dijiye. 🤭

Aap pehle. Hamesha. 🤍
