While Having Breakfast

Meri jaan ☕🫂💕

Sabse pehle ek important sawaal... aapne breakfast kiya ya nahi? 👀

Aur please mujhe ye mat bolna ki “baad me kar lunga” 😭 Kyunki aapki ye aadat mujhe bilkul pasand nahi hai. 🤭

Abhi properly baithiye, kuch achha sa khaiye, aur araam se breakfast kijiye. 🫂

Kaash main abhi aapke saamne baithi hoti... toh main aapko dekhte hue apna breakfast karti aur beech beech me aapko tang bhi karti. 😂💕

Aur agar aap kuch tasty kha rahe hain na, toh ek bite meri taraf se bhi. 🤭 Haan, imaginary hi sahi. 😂🫂

Waise mujhe lagta hai breakfast ke time hum dono ki bohot bak-bak hoti. 😭 Aap apni random stories sunate, main beech me interrupt karti, aur phir pata hi nahi chalta breakfast kab khatam ho gaya. 🤭💕

Mujhe humare aise simple moments bohot pasand aayenge. Koi fancy date nahi, koi special place nahi... bas hum dono, ek table, thoda sa khana, aur bohot saari baatein. 🥹🤍

Aur haan, breakfast skip karne ki permission bilkul nahi hai. 😤😂

Aapko healthy rehna hai, khush rehna hai, aur apni mumma ki baat bhi maan-ni hai. 🤭🫂

Toh ab phone thoda side me rakhiye, properly breakfast kijiye aur enjoy kijiye apni morning. ☕💕

Aur jab breakfast karte hue ek second ke liye meri yaad aaye... toh bas ek cute si smile kar dena. 🤭🫂

Main samajh jaungi. 💗

Muaaaaaaw 💋

Ab jao meri jaan, breakfast finish karo. 😗🫂💕

With all my love,

🤍 Mummaa
