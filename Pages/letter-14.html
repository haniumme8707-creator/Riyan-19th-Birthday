When You Want To Feel Loved

Meri jaan, mera shona 🫂💕

Agar aapne ye letter khola hai, toh aaj aapko meri taraf se thoda extra pyaar chahiye. 🤭

Toh aaiye... mere paas baithiye. Bilkul paas. 🫂

Main aapko ek baat batana chahti hu— aapko mere pyaar ko earn karne ki kabhi zarurat nahi hai.

Aapko perfect hone ki zarurat nahi, har waqt happy rehne ki zarurat nahi, aur har cheez sahi karne ki bhi zarurat nahi.

Aap bas aap rahiye... aur mujhe aap waise hi pasand hain. 🤍

Aapke ache days me main aapke saath hu, aur jab din thoda difficult ho, tab bhi main aapke saath hu.

Aapki khushi mujhe genuinely khush karti hai, aur aapka udaas hona mujhe aapko aur tight hug karne ka mann karata hai. 🥺🫂

Isliye jab bhi aapko lage ki aapko pyaar chahiye, bas ye yaad rakhiye—

Kahin na kahin, koi aapko bohot, bohot zyada pyaar karta hai. 💕

Aur haan... woh “koi” main hu. 🤭💋

Ab ek tight hug le lijiye. 🫂🫂 Aur ek kiss bhi. 💋

Muaaaaaaw meri jaan. 💕

With all my love,

🤍 Mummaa
